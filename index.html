About

I am a Ph.D. student in the Department of Electronic Engineering at Tsinghua University, advised by Assoc. Prof. Chao Zhang (Google Scholar). I received my B.Eng. from the same department in 2024.

I build multimodal foundation models. Most of my recent work teaches language models to read, reason about and forecast time series together with text and vision, and uses physical time series to ground video world models. I also work on speech generation and on aligning language models with human preferences.

Research

Time-series LLMs and world models

TimeOmni, a unified time-series LLM for understanding and generation; TimeOmni-v and the MMTA benchmark for time series, text and vision; TimeOmni-o1, which reasons in text and forecasts with a time-series foundation model; the time-series modality of Intern-S1 and Intern-S2; and Time-JEPA, which uses physical time series to supervise video world models.

Speech generation and speech LLMs

BELLE, a continuous-valued TTS model that uses Bayesian evidential learning to capture the one-to-many uncertainty of speech; and TVPara, data for paralinguistic understanding in speech LLMs.

LLM alignment

Bayesian WeakS-to-Strong, which extends weak-to-strong generalization to an ensemble of weak supervisors and to generation with preference learning.
