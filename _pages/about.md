I am a Ph.D. student in the Department of Electronic Engineering at [Tsinghua University](https://www.tsinghua.edu.cn/en/), advised by Assoc. Prof. [Chao Zhang](https://mi.eng.cam.ac.uk/~cz277/) ([Google Scholar](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=Imkhv-YAAAAJ)). I received my B.Eng. from the same department in 2024.

My research builds multimodal foundation models along three lines:

- **Time-series LLMs and world models.** Models that read, reason about and forecast time series together with text and vision (TimeOmni, TimeOmni-v, TimeOmni-o1, the Intern-S1/S2 time-series modality), benchmarks for them (SciTS, MMTA), and physical time series as supervision for video world models (Time-JEPA).
- **Speech generation and speech LLMs.** Continuous-valued TTS that models the one-to-many uncertainty of speech (BELLE), and data for paralinguistic understanding in speech LLMs (TVPara).
- **LLM alignment.** Weak-to-strong generalization and preference learning (Bayesian WeakS-to-Strong).
